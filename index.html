Support

Sorello is an offline English dictation keyboard and encrypted voice notes app for iPhone, requiring iOS 26 or later. It was previously called LocalFlow.

Setup

  1. Open Sorello and complete onboarding, including microphone permission and local speech model preparation.
  2. In Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards, tap Add New Keyboard and choose Sorello. Then tap Sorello and turn on Allow Full Access so the keyboard can communicate with Sorello on your iPhone.
  3. In any text field, for example in Notes, switch to Sorello with the globe key.
  4. Tap Start to dictate and tap Stop to insert the text. By default, Sorello dictates directly inside the keyboard when no app session is running. If you prefer app-managed sessions—or want to use your Custom Dictionary while dictating—start a session in Sorello (or with the Dictation Session control in Control Center or on the Action button), or turn off Dictate in the keyboard in Sorello › Diagnostics.
  5. To record private voice notes, open Notes in Sorello, set up your vault (requires an iPhone passcode), and optionally save your recovery code. You can start a voice note from Notes, from an active session’s Live Activity, or from the Voice Note control.

Common questions

Why is the orange microphone indicator on before I tap Start? When both Dictate in the keyboard (on by default) and Use iPhone microphone (on by default) are enabled and Bluetooth headphones are connected with no other app playing audio, opening the Sorello keyboard holds the microphone open to keep audio routed to your iPhone’s built-in mic instead of switching slowly from your headphones on tap. Audio is not processed, transcribed, or stored until you tap Start. Dismissing or switching away from the keyboard ends this hold immediately. To turn off pre-holding, switch off Use iPhone microphone on Sorello’s Home screen, or switch off Dictate in the keyboard in Sorello › Diagnostics.

Why is the microphone indicator still on after I close the keyboard? An active app dictation session or an ongoing voice note keeps the microphone open in the background. Audio between dictations is discarded unconverted. Tap End session in Sorello (or use the Dictation Session control) to stop a session immediately; sessions also end automatically after the idle timeout set in Sorello (5 minutes by default). To stop a voice note, tap Stop in Sorello, on the Live Activity, on the keyboard bar, or with the Voice Note control.

Why does Sorello use my iPhone microphone instead of my Bluetooth headphones? Use iPhone microphone is on by default in Sorello › Dictation so your headphones stay on higher-quality audio during sessions and dictation captures clearer speech. Turn it off on Sorello’s Home screen if you want iOS to use your Bluetooth headset microphone instead.

How are Voice Notes protected, and how do I move them to a new iPhone? Voice notes store text only (never audio), encrypted on your iPhone and locked behind Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. Your encrypted notes are included in standard iOS/iCloud backups, but the device encryption key stays in this iPhone’s Keychain only. Create a recovery code in Notes › Settings and store it safely—it is the only way to unlock restored notes on a new iPhone or if you turn off your device passcode. Sorello cannot recover a lost code. There is no built-in file export; to copy a note out, unlock it and select and copy its text.

Why is the keyboard unavailable in some fields? iOS requires the system keyboard for password and other secure fields, and individual apps can disable custom keyboards.

Why is there no inserted text? Check that microphone permission and Full Access are enabled, wait for speech model preparation to finish, and try a short dictation in Apple Notes. Locking your iPhone cancels an in-progress keyboard dictation (voice notes continue recording while locked). If you switched fields before transcription finished, tap Insert last dictation on the keyboard bar within 60 seconds.

Report a problem

In Sorello, choose Report a bug > Write email, or email support@audeolabs.com directly. Include steps to reproduce, what you expected, what happened, your app version and build, iOS version, and device type. Avoid including passwords, sensitive text, or personal recordings. No recordings, transcripts, notes, diagnostics, or logs are attached by the app automatically.
